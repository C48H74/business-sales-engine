# Website and decision-support content

Use [pitch narrative](../templates/pitch-narrative.md), [case study](../templates/case-study.md) and [AI brand audit](../templates/ai-brand-audit.md). V1's Crestodina influence is retained in the [reading map](reading-by-section.md); no universal buyer-readiness percentage is claimed.

Inspect actual buyer questions and the distribution/conversion bottleneck. If prospects cannot understand scope, fit, price, proof or next steps, improve key pages and decision aids. If good-fit visitors convert but volume is too low, reach/distribution may deserve attention concurrently.

Map each content item to a buyer situation, unresolved question, supported claim and next action. Useful decision aids include comparison with alternatives, implementation effort, exclusions, sample deliverables and pricing assumptions. Top-of-funnel content is appropriate when building relevant future demand, not prohibited until every buying page is perfect.

Teach verified practice or explicitly identified research/hypotheses. Separate own experience, cited external experience and simulations. Titles must match content. Attribute expert contributions and secure any needed permissions. Avoid guarantees that search rankings or assistant citations will follow publication.

Set a cadence based on capacity; update outdated information and broken proof before adding repetitive posts. Track qualified conversations, assisted decisions, customer usefulness and contribution where attribution permits; do not infer causality from a page view.

Content experiment: one audience/question, baseline, artifact, distribution route, observation window, outcome and guardrails. Use [experiment card](../templates/experiment-card.md). AI can help analyze questions and draft from approved sources, but a synthetic persona response is not customer research. See [AI sales](ai-sales.md).
