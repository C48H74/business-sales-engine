# Signal-based outbound and deliverability

Use [five touches](../templates/5-touch-sequence.md), [cold calls](../templates/cold-call-scripts.md), [email examples](../examples/cold-email-good-vs-bad.md). Public hiring, announced expansion, consented usage or a requested deadline may be signals, not proof of pain, budget or intent.

Record source/date, observed fact, relevance hypothesis, confidence, contact fit and channel constraints. Exclude sensitive personal inferences. Personalization connects a verified business fact to a useful question; avoid flattery or fake familiarity.

Message: observed signal → tentative problem → bounded help/proof → one easy action. Do not invent cold-email ROI. Someone else's public case is not your result. Five touches are a configurable example, not a quota. Stop on refusal, opt-out, wrong recipient, account suppression or reputation issues; do not evade refusal by switching channels.

Before sending, verify provider rules and applicable market/channel requirements. Gmail distinguishes all-sender rules from stronger personal-Gmail bulk requirements involving SPF/DKIM, DMARC alignment and unsubscribe. Its FAQ reports stronger enforcement from November 2025. Technical compliance does not grant permission for unsolicited outreach. [S7/S8](sources.md)

Check authentication, identity, replies/opt-outs, suppression, list quality, bounces and complaints. Gradual sending does not guarantee delivery. Avoid artificial warm-up networks and rotating domains to evade blocks. Agree campaign-specific guardrails with the sender owner; pause deterioration and diagnose.

Measure delivered messages, relevant replies, qualified opportunities, cost and retained value. Opens are weak evidence due to scanners/privacy tools. Use comparable cohorts or a holdout. AI relevance is a testable hypothesis, not justification for more volume.
