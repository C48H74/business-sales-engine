# SaaS, PLG and PQLs

Use [PLG experiment](../templates/plg-experiment.md), [dashboard](../templates/metrics-dashboard.md), [SaaS case](../examples/saas-plg-case.md). ProductLed describes PQLs around experienced value and usage [S9](sources.md). Thresholds below must be fitted to the product.

Choose freemium, trial, sandbox, paid pilot or sales-led evaluation. Freemium reduces entry risk but adds hosting/support/abuse costs and may not fit costly setup or sensitive production integration.

Define the first useful completed customer job, not login. Instrument event, eligible-account denominator, time-to-value, activation window and repeat use. In-product onboarding should guide that result and explain limits.

Candidate PQL = demonstrated value + account fit + legitimate evaluation/expansion signal. Example hypothesis: two collaborators repeatedly completing the workflow plus need for controlled team access. Validate paid conversion, retained contribution and false positives before trusting the signal. Follow consented sales-assist routes; do not contact every active user.

Expansion should add useful capacity, seats, workflows or support. Do not withdraw promised access or obstruct export to force upgrades. Explain price/usage thresholds before billing. Upsell may become substantial; it is not every SaaS's principal revenue source.

Track cohort activation/conversion, customer churn, GRR, NRR, CAC payback and free-user cost. Separate users/accounts and periods. Strong NRR can conceal departing small customers. Prioritize retention when durable value or new-cohort economics fail; otherwise compare marginal returns rather than enforcing retention above acquisition in every case. See [metrics](metrics.md) and [AI evaluation](ai-sales.md).
