# Pricing and billing decisions

Use [offer/pricing canvas](../templates/offer-pricing-canvas.md), [economics](economics.md) and [numerical examples](../examples/unit-economics.md). Stark [S16](sources.md) provides a value-pricing lens; the decision matrix here is original synthesis.

Price using delivery/support cost, capacity, risk, alternatives, buyer value and actual willingness to commit. A cost floor is not proof of market demand. Value should distinguish total potential, attributable improvement and realistically captured benefit. Do not sell projected customer savings as guaranteed revenue.

| Model | Good fit | Important control |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed project | Bounded scope and acceptance | Dependencies, change requests, rework |
| Time/materials | Uncertain workload or staff augmentation | Rate, estimate range, cap/review cadence |
| Retainer | Defined ongoing access/obligation | Capacity, response limits, renewal |
| Subscription | Continuing repeatable value | Entitlements, cancellation, cohort retention |
| Usage | Measurable value-correlated consumption | Transparent meter, alerts, bill limits |
| Performance | Credible measurable attribution | Baseline, verification, settlement and downside |

Hourly billing can be appropriate; do not choose it just by converting an old salary or prohibit it universally. Performance pricing is not itself a guarantee of performance.

Publish standard prices or honest ranges when they help qualification. Complex quotes need scope/terms; discovery should not become a barrier to transparency. Explain taxes/exclusions where applicable, without fabricating local tax advice.

Pricing experiments specify cohort, offer version, guardrails and capacity. Compare contribution and retention as well as close rate. Test alternatives sequentially if samples are too small for useful randomization. Do not disguise arbitrary prices as a validated test.

Package design is optional. The same 100 customers at 90 × base plus 10 × ten-times-base produce 1.9 times baseline revenue. Profit depends on acquisition/service mix and can fall even when revenue rises. See [negotiation](negotiation.md) before conceding price.
