# Ethical persuasion and transparent limits

Cialdini identifies influence mechanisms [S10](sources.md). Applications below are original safeguards, not persuasion guarantees.

| Principle | Appropriate use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Reciprocity | Useful diagnostic without obligation | Gifts followed by pressure |
| Commitment/consistency | Follow buyer-confirmed goals | Treat exploration as consent |
| Social proof | Relevant approved cases with limitations | Fake testimonials/logos |
| Authority | Verified relevant expertise | Borrowed prestige/credentials |
| Liking | Respect and genuine common ground | Fake familiarity |
| Scarcity | Verified capacity/limited stock | Reset deadlines/fictional slots |
| Unity | Real shared purpose | Identity exploitation/exclusion |

Identify the risk: money, migration, reputation, disruption or unsupported outcomes. Match sample, pilot, acceptance test, rollback, support or bounded guarantee to it. A refund does not restore downtime.

Define covered failure, conditions, remedy, duration, exclusions, maximum exposure and owner before promising. Model expected refunds/rework and worst-case cash need. Stacking is optional; contradictory or unfundable commitments hurt trust.

State who should not buy, required dependencies, limitations and where cheaper alternatives suffice. Example: "This model audit does not replace design approval or certify the plant." Match urgency to dated capacity or external evidence, offer the next option and allow refusal. Use [risk review](../checklists/risk-and-proof.md).
