## Claim discipline

Observed repository/source facts, author/framework interpretations and this skill's proposals are different categories. Provider requirements can change. No fixed success rate, committee size, talk ratio, "97% not ready", guaranteed AI lift or premium profit share is asserted. Examples are synthetic. Validate customer facts, law-sensitive execution and vendor requirements in the actual task context.
