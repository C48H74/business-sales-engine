## Review procedure

1. Assign a maintainer and actual review date. Open the cited provider/author pages and relevant new primary sources; record what was checked, including unchanged items.
2. For benchmarks record population, segment, sample period, methodology and cost definitions. Reject a percentage without a denominator/source.
3. Update affected references/templates and run link/frontmatter/numerical validation. Add a changelog entry for changed behavior, not just the date.
4. If fresh evidence is unavailable, mark that item unverified/stale. Do not advance its review date as though it was checked.

Next-review is a reminder, not an installed scheduled automation. No claim is made that an entire referenced book was read in preparing this version.
